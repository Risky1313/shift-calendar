★ Work Shifts

Rotation, paydays and holidays for Apple Calendar

Subscribe in Apple Calendar or download the file once
How it works
  1. Tap Subscribe in Apple Calendar, then Subscribe, then Add.
  2. Your shifts, paydays and holidays show up and stay up to date by themselves.
  3. Each shift has a 1-hour reminder. On iPhone, check that Remove Alerts is off for this calendar if you want them.
